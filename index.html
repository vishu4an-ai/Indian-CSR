        What the numbers mean

        • The main figure for each company is its FY25 CSR spend under Section 135 as given by PRIME Infobase (drawn from company filings) or the company's annual report. Where a source gave something else, the row says so and carries a "Check basis" tag.
        • Group, standalone, spent and obligation figures are different measures. They are shown as reported and never converted.
        • Sector groups and the PSU tag are my own labels for readability, not categories from any source. Energy includes oil, gas, power and coal.

        What could not be done

        • The list stops at 40 because that is how many companies had a figure that could be verified from a published source. A ranked top 100 was not reachable.
        • Some publishers block automated reading, and the MCA portal and Dataful dashboards did not load. Figures from those came from the excerpts shown in search results and link to the page. The sector table is partial for that reason.
        • Fields with no published value read "Not found in the sources reviewed". Nothing was estimated to fill them.
